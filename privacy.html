Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PetPassport ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using PetPassport, you agree to the practices described below.

Who we are

PetPassport is operated by Justin Hui ("we"). Contact: jthcreative12@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (if provided through Google Sign-In)
  • Profile photo URL (if provided through Google Sign-In)
  • A unique account identifier generated by Firebase Authentication

If you sign in with Google, we receive only the basic profile information you authorize Google to share with us. We do not receive your Google password.

Pet and health information you provide

You may add information about your pets to the app, including:

  • Pet name, species, breed, date of birth, and other identifying details
  • Photos you upload from your device's camera or photo library
  • Vaccination records, vet visit notes, medications, and other health-related information you choose to enter
  • Identification details (microchip number, license number, etc.)

This information is provided voluntarily by you. We treat it as your data — we do not analyze it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties except as described below.

Push notification token

If you enable notifications, we collect a device-specific push notification token so we can send you reminders and updates you've opted into (for example, vaccination due-date reminders). You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Diagnostic and usage information

We collect limited technical information to keep the app running reliably, including app version, device model, operating system version, and crash reports. This information is not tied to your personal identity beyond what is necessary to debug an issue you've reported.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core functionality of the app (storing and displaying your pet records, syncing across your devices)
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To send you push notifications you've opted into
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To diagnose and fix bugs and improve app stability

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the app.

Where your information is stored

Your account and pet data are stored using Google Firebase services (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Messaging) operated by Google LLC. Data may be processed and stored on Google's servers in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Third-party services we use

We use the following third-party services to operate the app. Each is bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and notification delivery. Privacy policy
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. Privacy policy
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — delivery of notifications on iOS. Privacy policy

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app
  • If required by law, court order, or valid legal process
  • To protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others

Your rights and choices

Access and update

You can view and edit your pet information at any time inside the app.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). When you delete your account, we permanently remove your account record and your pet data from our active databases. Backups containing your data are removed within 30 days. To request manual deletion, email support@yourdomain.com.

Notifications

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's iOS Settings under Notifications → PetPassport.

Camera and photo library access

The app requests permission to access your camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a pet profile. You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → PetPassport.

Data retention

We retain your account and pet data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete the associated data as described above. Crash and diagnostic logs are retained for up to 90 days.

Children's privacy

PetPassport is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@yourdomain.com and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), authentication of all account access, and reliance on Google Firebase's industry-standard infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International users

By using PetPassport, you understand that your information will be transferred to and stored in countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence, including the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after the effective date of an updated policy constitutes acceptance of the changes.
