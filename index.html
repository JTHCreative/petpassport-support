Frequently asked questions

How do I add a pet?

Open the app, tap the + icon on the home screen, and follow the prompts to add your pet's name, photo, and basic info.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your pet's information is stored in your personal account and is never sold or shared. See our privacy policy.
